# Coaching

Coaching is an add-on. Where your organisation has it, **Coaching** appears in the left sidebar and agents get a portal of their own.

---

## What It Does

- **Courses**: build training around a gap in one category, and set the score range there that assigns it. Agents are reached by score rather than by name.
- **Progress**: see who has started, finished, or stalled, and what they scored.
- **Awards**: define a score range in a category that earns recognition, and Vela presents it on each evaluation cycle.
- **Preferences**: set the evaluation cycle, the pass mark, and how much of their own work agents see.

Agents sign in to the Agent Portal to work through what they have been assigned.
