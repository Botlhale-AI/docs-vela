# Configure Your Notifications

Vela's notification system keeps you informed about important events. Configure your preferences to receive the right notifications at the right time, whether on the platform or via email.

## What You Can Achieve

By configuring your notifications properly, you'll be able to:

| **Stay Informed** | **Team Collaboration** | **Timely Updates** |
|-------------------|----------------------|-------------------|
| Receive important alerts when they happen | Get notified about team comments and discussions | Choose between real-time or daily updates |
| Never miss critical events | Stay updated on mentions and activity | Control how often you receive notifications |
| Customise what you're notified about | Track new reports and alerts | Manage notification overload |

---

## Notification Types

Based on the actual Vela interface, you can configure notifications for these five key areas:

### **Comments**
Get notified about comments made on calls, interactions, or team discussions. This helps you stay updated on team feedback and insights.

### **Activity On Your Comments**
Receive notifications when someone responds to or interacts with comments you've made. This keeps you engaged in ongoing discussions.

### **Comments Mentioning You**
Stay informed when team members specifically mention you in comments using @username. This ensures you don't miss important references or questions directed at you.

### **New Reports**
Get notified when new reports are generated and available for review. This helps you stay on top of performance analytics and insights.

### **New Alerts Detected**
Receive notifications when new alerts are triggered by your Smart Search patterns or other monitoring systems. This ensures you respond to issues promptly.

## Getting Started with Notifications

### Step 1: Access Notification Settings
- [ ] **Log in** to your Vela dashboard
- [ ] **Go to "Settings" → "Notifications"** (or similar settings path)
- [ ] **You'll see two main sections**:
  - [ ] **Platform Notifications** - In-app notifications
  - [ ] **Email Notifications** - Email delivery

### Step 2: Configure Platform Notifications
- [ ] **Check the boxes** for notifications you want to receive on the platform:
  - [ ] **Comments** - Get notified about new comments
  - [ ] **Activity On Your Comments** - When someone responds to your comments
  - [ ] **Comments Mentioning You** - When you're mentioned in comments
  - [ ] **New Reports** - When new reports are available
  - [ ] **New Alerts Detected** - When new alerts are triggered

### Step 3: Configure Email Notifications
- [ ] **Check the boxes** for notifications you want to receive via email:
  - [ ] **Comments** - Email notifications for new comments
  - [ ] **Activity On Your Comments** - Email when someone responds to your comments
  - [ ] **Comments Mentioning You** - Email when you're mentioned
  - [ ] **New Reports** - Email when new reports are available
  - [ ] **New Alerts Detected** - Email when new alerts are triggered

### Step 4: Set Email Frequency
- [ ] **Choose your email notification frequency**:
  - [ ] **Real-Time** - Receive emails immediately when events occur
  - [ ] **Daily** - Receive a daily digest of all notifications

### Step 5: Save Your Settings
- [ ] **Click "Save"** to apply your notification preferences

## Understanding Your Notification Options

### Platform vs Email Notifications
You can choose to receive notifications in two ways:

| **Platform Notifications** | **Email Notifications** |
|---------------------------|------------------------|
| Appear within the Vela platform | Delivered to your email inbox |
| Real-time delivery | Can be real-time or daily digest |
| Don't require email access | Accessible from anywhere |
| Less intrusive | More persistent |

### Email Frequency Options
Choose how often you want to receive email notifications:

| **Real-Time** | **Daily** |
|---------------|-----------|
| Receive emails immediately when events occur | Receive one daily digest with all notifications |
| Best for critical alerts and urgent matters | Best for general updates and reducing email overload |
| More email volume | Less email volume |
| Immediate awareness | Scheduled review time |

### Notification Types Explained
Each notification type serves a specific purpose:

- **Comments**: Stay updated on team discussions and feedback
- **Activity On Your Comments**: Know when your contributions get responses
- **Comments Mentioning You**: Don't miss direct references or questions
- **New Reports**: Be notified when fresh analytics are available
- **New Alerts Detected**: Respond quickly to important events

## Best Practices for Notification Management

### Choosing the Right Settings
- [ ] **Start with essential notifications** - Enable only what you need to stay informed
- [ ] **Use real-time for critical alerts** - Get immediate notifications for urgent matters
- [ ] **Enable platform notifications** - Stay updated when you're actively using Vela

### Managing Notification Overload
- [ ] **Review your settings regularly** - Adjust based on what you actually need
- [ ] **Disable notifications you don't use** - Reduce unnecessary interruptions
- [ ] **Focus on mentions and alerts** - Prioritise notifications that require action

### Team Communication
- [ ] **Enable comment notifications** - Stay engaged with team discussions
- [ ] **Turn on mention notifications** - Don't miss direct references to you
- [ ] **Use @mentions strategically** - Tag people when you need their attention
- [ ] **Respond to comment activity** - Keep discussions active and productive
