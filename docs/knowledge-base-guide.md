# Build Your Team's Knowledge Base

Upload your company's knowledge base and organisational information to enhance Vela's AI analysis. This helps our LLM understand your business context and provide more accurate call analysis and agent performance assessment.

## What This Does

When you upload your knowledge base, Vela's AI will:
- Understand your specific business terminology and processes
- Provide more accurate analysis of your calls
- Assess agent performance based on your actual business standards
- Give better coaching recommendations tailored to your industry

---

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Access Knowledge Base
- [ ] **Go to "Smart Detector" → "Knowledge Base"**
- [ ] **Click "Upload"** to start

### Step 2: Upload Your Information
- [ ] **Upload your company documents** (PDFs, text files, URLs)
- [ ] **Add your organisational bio** - company overview, mission, values
- [ ] **Include industry-specific information** to help our LLM understand your business

---

## What to Upload

### Company Documents
Upload these types of documents to help our AI understand your business:

- **Company policies and procedures** - HR policies, customer service procedures, escalation processes
- **Product information** - Features, specifications, pricing, troubleshooting guides
- **Industry information** - Terminology, regulatory requirements, common issues
- **Training materials** - Scripts, best practices, compliance guidelines

### Organisational Bio
Include information about your company:

- **Company mission and values** - What your business stands for
- **Business model** - How you operate and make money
- **Target market** - Who your customers are
- **Key metrics** - What success looks like for your business

---

## Best Practices

### Content Quality
- [ ] **Use your actual business terminology** - Don't use generic language
- [ ] **Include current information** - Update when your business changes
- [ ] **Be specific** - Include real examples from your business
- [ ] **Keep it relevant** - Focus on information that affects your calls


---

## How It Works

When you upload your knowledge base, our AI will:

- **Understand your business context** - Recognise your industry terminology and processes
- **Provide more accurate analysis** - Better identify real issues vs false positives
- **Assess agents properly** - Evaluate performance against your actual standards
- **Give relevant coaching** - Recommendations that match your business needs

### Keep It Updated
- [ ] **Review monthly** - Check if AI analysis reflects your business reality
- [ ] **Update when needed** - Add new information as your business changes
- [ ] **Test improvements** - See if updates make analysis more accurate


---

## Troubleshooting

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
|-------------|--------------|
| **AI analysis seems generic** | Upload more specific business information and terminology |
| **Agent scoring doesn't match your standards** | Include your actual performance criteria and procedures |
| **Missing industry-specific insights** | Add industry terminology and common issues |
| **Coaching recommendations not relevant** | Provide clear business objectives and success metrics |
