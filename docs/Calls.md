# Calls

Calls are your voice interactions. Upload a recording and Vela transcribes it and analyses the conversation, so you can review performance and coach your agents.

---

## What It Does

Each processed call includes a summary, the sentiment through the conversation, the Agent Scorecard outcome, and any alerts a Smart Search raised. It also surfaces keywords, the customer's intent, and pain points, with timestamps on every line.

---

## How It Works

- Upload a single call (WAV or MP3), or many at once in a ZIP with a metadata CSV.
- Vela processes the call in the background and emails you when the analysis is ready, depending on your notification settings.
- Open the interaction to read the transcript alongside the analysis, override any scorecard outcome, and leave coaching comments.
