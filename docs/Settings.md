# Settings

Settings is where administrators configure the organisation, and where every user manages their own account.

---

## What You Configure

- **Users and teams**: add people, build your department and team structure, and set each person's role and access level.
- **Data privacy**: choose which entity types Vela redacts from transcripts, and who can view the unredacted version.
- **Notifications**: choose which in-app and email notifications you receive.
- **Account and security**: manage your own profile and password.
