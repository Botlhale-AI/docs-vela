# Quick Start Guide for Agents

Welcome to **Vela Agent Portal**!  
The Agent Portal helps you understand your performance, receive feedback from your team lead, and access courses to improve your skills.  

This guide will get you comfortable using your portal in just **15 minutes**, so you can start tracking your progress and responding to coaching right away.

---

## Getting Started

### System Requirements
- **Supported browsers**: Chrome, Firefox, Edge  
- **Internet connection**: Required to access your portal  

### Logging Into Vela
Your team lead or administrator has set up your account. Here's how to access it:

1. Go to your Vela login page (provided by your team lead).  
2. Choose your login method:  
   - **Google**: Sign in with Google  
   - **Microsoft**: Sign in with Microsoft  
   - **Email and Password**: Use the credentials your team lead gave you  
3. First-time setup:  
   - Verify your email  
   - Create a strong password (8+ characters, mix of letters, numbers, special characters)

![Agent login page](../../../img/screenshots/settings/agent-login.png)

💡 **Tip:** Having trouble logging in? Contact your team lead for a reset or verification.

---

## Agent Portal Overview

After login, you’ll see a **simplified dashboard** built just for agents.  

### Main Sections
- **Dashboard** – Your performance scores and trends  
- **My Interactions** – Your calls and chats with feedback  
- **Courses** – Training modules assigned to you  
- **Awards** – Recognition for achievements  
- **Notifications** – Messages from your team lead  

![Agent portal navigation](../../../img/screenshots/settings/agent-portal-nav.png)

🔒 You only see your own data. All performance data is private.

---

## Essential First Steps

### 1. Check Your Performance Dashboard (5 min)
See your **overall score**, **trends**, **peer comparisons**, and **recent activity**.  

![Agent dashboard](../../../img/screenshots/dashboard/agent-dashboard.png)

- **Green** = Excellent performance  
- **Yellow** = Good, but room for growth  
- **Red** = Needs attention  

**Outcome:** You’ll know exactly how you’re doing and where to focus.

---

### 2. Review Your Interactions and Feedback (5 min)
1. Go to **My Interactions**  
2. Select a call or chat  
3. Look for the comment icon to read feedback  
4. Respond with questions or confirmation  

![Interaction list with comments](../../../img/screenshots/calls/agent-interaction.png)

💬 **Feedback types:**  
- Positive recognition  
- Coaching tips  
- Course recommendations  

**Outcome:** You’ll stay connected with your coach and improve faster.

---

### 3. Complete Your Assigned Courses (5 min)
- Open **Courses**  
- Start an **Assigned Course**  
- Track progress with **In Progress / Completed** tabs  
- Earn **Certificates**  

![Courses assigned](../../../img/screenshots/courses/agent-courses.png)

**Outcome:** You’ll continuously improve your skills and boost your scores.

---

## Daily Routine for Success

- **Start shift**: Check dashboard + notifications  
- **Breaks**: Review feedback, do short training modules  
- **End shift**: Quick score review, reply to feedback, plan improvements

## Understanding Performance

### What Vela Measures
- Customer service quality  
- Communication skills  
- Problem resolution  
- Professionalism  
- Compliance  

### How Scores Work
- AI evaluates interactions  
- Team leads may adjust  
- Overall score = rolling average  
- **Focus on trends, not single calls**

## Troubleshooting

- **Login issues** → Contact team lead  
- **Course won’t load** → Refresh or change browser  
- **Scores not updating** → Wait a few hours

## Privacy

- You only see **your own interactions and scores**  
- Peer comparisons are anonymous  
- Team leads see your scores, interactions, and training progress  
- Data is only used for coaching and development
