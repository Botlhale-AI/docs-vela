# Platform Overview

## What is Vela?

Vela is a comprehensive call centre analytics platform that transforms how organisations approach quality assurance, agent development, and customer experience optimisation. By leveraging advanced AI technology, Vela analyses 100% of your customer interactions regardless of language or interaction type(calls or chats). It provides actionable insights that drive measurable improvements in service quality and operational efficiency.

### The Three Pillars of Vela

Vela is built around three core capabilities that work together to elevate your call centre operations:

#### 1. 100% Quality Assurance

Unlike traditional QA approaches that rely on sampling a small percentage of interactions, Vela analyzes every call and chat conversation automatically. This comprehensive coverage means:

- **No blind spots** - Every customer interaction is evaluated for quality and compliance
- **Complete visibility** - Identify patterns and trends across all agents and teams
- **Objective evaluation** - AI-powered scoring provides consistent, unbiased assessment

#### 2. Agent Coaching & Development

Vela transforms QA data into personalised development programs that help agents continuously improve:

- **Data-driven coaching** - Identify specific skill gaps with precision
- **Automated training assignment** - Courses automatically assigned based on performance needs
- **Progress tracking** - Monitor improvement over time with clear metrics
- **Recognition programs** - Celebrate achievements and motivate excellence

#### 3. Business Insights

Beyond individual performance, Vela provides strategic intelligence that informs business decisions:

- **Customer sentiment analysis** - Understand how customers feel about your service
- **Pain point identification** - Discover recurring customer frustrations
- **Operational trends** - Identify patterns affecting efficiency and satisfaction
- **Predictive analytics** - Anticipate issues before they escalate

## What Makes Vela Different

### Multilingual Excellence

Vela supports transcription and analysis in **multiple languages, including African languages** such as IsiXhosa,Sesotho and Afrikaans that many competitors don't support. This capability is essential for organisations serving diverse markets and ensures quality standards are maintained across all customer segments.

### End-to-End Coaching Workflow

While most analytics platforms stop at reporting, Vela completes the entire quality improvement cycle:

**Traditional Platforms:** Analyse → Report → (Manual follow-up)

**Vela:** Analyse → Report → Coach → Train → Track → Recognise → Improve

This integrated approach ensures insights actually translate into performance improvements.

### AI-Powered, Human-Centric Design

Vela's AI doesn't replace your expertise—it enhances it. The platform provides intelligent recommendations and automation while keeping you in control:

- **AI suggestions, human decisions** - Technology supports your judgment, doesn't override it
- **Customisable evaluation criteria** - Define quality standards that match your business
- **Manual override capabilities** - Adjust AI assessments when context requires it
- **Collaborative tools** - Facilitate human coaching and development conversations

### Unified Call and Chat Analytics

Many tools specialise in either voice or digital channels. Vela provides a single, unified view of customer interactions across:

- **Voice calls** (inbound and outbound)
- **Chat conversations** (web chat, messaging platforms)

This comprehensive visibility ensures consistent quality standards regardless of channel.

### Knowledge Base Integration

Vela uses your organisation's specific "knowledge base" procedures, products and policies to provide more accurate and contextually relevant scoring. This means evaluations reflect your unique business requirements, not generic industry standards.

## How Vela Works

### 1. Data Collection

**Upload interactions through multiple methods:**
- Single call/chat upload for immediate analysis
- Bulk upload for processing large volumes (up to 50GB batches)
- API integration for automated data flow
- Supported formats: WAV, MP3 for audio; JSON for chats

### 2. AI Processing

**Automatic analysis of every interaction:**
- **Transcription** - Convert speech to text in multiple languages
- **Speaker identification** - Separate agent and customer speech
- **Sentiment analysis** - Track emotional tone throughout conversation
- **Keyword detection** - Identify important terms and compliance phrases
- **Intent classification** - Understand customer goals and needs
- **Pain point identification** - Detect customer frustration indicators
- **Automatic scoring** - Evaluate performance against quality criteria

### 3. Quality Assurance

**Human review enhanced by AI insights:**
- Review AI-generated scorecards and analysis
- Listen to calls or read chat transcripts
- Add manual evaluations and coaching comments
- Flag interactions for follow-up or escalation
- Track compliance and procedure adherence

### 4. Coaching & Development

**Turn insights into improvement:**
- Identify skill gaps from performance data
- Assign relevant training courses automatically
- Provide specific, timely feedback to agents
- Track improvement over time
- Recognise achievements and progress

### 5. Reporting & Analysis

**Demonstrate impact and identify opportunities:**
- Generate performance reports for stakeholders
- Identify trends and patterns across teams
- Receive AI recommendations for improvement
- Make data-driven operational decisions
- Track ROI of quality initiatives
